# Akari V2.2 Milky-Pop Cat-Ear Study Design

## Goal

Create twelve noncanonical Akari V2.2 illustrations that push the earlier
cat-ear study toward a brighter pop-anime finish, much stronger camera energy,
and a luminous milky-pastel palette. The character must read as canonical adult
Akari first and as an intentionally playful cat-ear fantasy second.

## Output Boundary

- Produce twelve portrait PNG candidates targeted at 1024 x 1536.
- Store them under
  `tmp/akari-v2.2-milky-pop-cat-ear-study-20260811/r01/`.
- Preserve the exact final prompts, source paths, per-image QA, and a 4 x 3
  contact sheet beside the candidates.
- Keep the batch ignored, untracked, and noncanonical until the user explicitly
  accepts an image.
- Do not assign Daily Scene numbers or modify the accepted V2.2 package during
  this exploration.

## Shared Character Lock

- Akari is a 25-year-old adult with her canonical warm chestnut hair and amber
  eyes.
- Exactly one filled blue capsule hairpin appears on canvas-right
  (Akari's anatomical left).
- Exactly one low side ponytail appears on the same canvas-right side and is
  tied with exactly one blue elastic. No braid, twin tails, second ponytail,
  second elastic, or second hairpin.
- Exactly two naturally grown cat ears emerge from the crown. Their chestnut
  outer fur and restrained warm-pink inner fur belong to Akari rather than to
  a headband or costume.
- The ears are deliberately asymmetric through tilt, outward opening, height
  caused by perspective, and tuft weight. They must still share a coherent
  crown placement and must not read as a missing, duplicated, or malformed ear.
- Human-ear positions remain fully covered by hair. No human ears and no tail
  are visible.
- No headband, seams, hardware, cap, collar, bell, paw clip, paw gloves,
  whiskers, animal nose, claws, or other animal traits.
- Adult proportions and modest adult clothing are mandatory. No mini skirt,
  cleavage, underwear emphasis, school uniform, or childlike age drift.

## Visual Language

- Use polished V2.2 anime rendering with a more graphic pop finish: clean
  linework, simplified broad color masses, refined amber eyes, warm cheeks,
  and crisp silhouette separation.
- Use luminous milky pastels rather than gray or chalky desaturation. Cream is
  the neutral; peach, mint, milk blue, lavender, butter yellow, and soft coral
  rotate as scene accents.
- Build dynamism through close wide-angle placement, oversized foreground
  hands or props, Dutch tilt, diagonal body flow, controlled crop, and motion
  arcs that arise from the physical action.
- Backgrounds may simplify real light, wind, reflections, or movement into
  original broad pastel bands and soft color shapes. They must not reproduce
  the supplied image's icons, exact peach outline, sticker border, symbol
  layout, or poster composition.
- The face stays immediately readable. Foreshortening must never break hand,
  limb, garment, prop, or room continuity.

## Twelve Scenes

### Close Face 01 — Cheek Press

An extreme close face crop with one connected index finger gently pressing one
cheek. The head tips into a peach-and-mint diagonal; one cat ear stands alert
while the other opens outward. Both amber eyes remain open, with a small
knowing smile rather than the supplied reference's wink.

### Close Face 02 — Bubble Lens

One large iridescent soap bubble occupies the near foreground and optically
magnifies part of one amber eye without covering the whole face. One connected
fingertip approaches the bubble without touching it. Milk blue, lavender, and
cream reflections create the pop color field.

### Close Face 03 — Cassette Secret

Akari holds one unlabeled translucent pastel cassette near the lower side of
her mouth as though sharing a secret. The cassette and one hand are enlarged
by the close lens; her face enters on the opposite diagonal. One ear turns
toward the cassette while the other leans toward the viewer.

### Close Face 04 — Sleepy Sleeve

In warm morning light, Akari rests her cheek against one oversized cream knit
sleeve while one relaxed hand emerges from its cuff. The close crop uses soft
butter and lavender slabs, half-lidded eyes, and uneven ear angles for a calm,
self-aware cute read without copying a paw pose.

### Foreground Half 05 — Sneaker Lace Pull

Seated on a simple entry bench, Akari pulls the two ends of one lace on one
mint-and-coral sneaker. The shoe dominates the low foreground while her face
leans close above it. Her other foot and the floor remain coherent; the outfit
uses a cream sweatshirt and adult lavender wide-leg trousers.

### Foreground Half 06 — Paper Plane Catch

One plain cream paper airplane arrives along a strong diagonal toward one open
foreground palm while Akari's other hand braces naturally beside her. She
leans from the opposite side with a delighted direct look. The background
reduces real window light and air movement into milk-blue and peach bands.

### Foreground Half 07 — Clear Umbrella Twirl

Akari rotates one transparent umbrella in light rain. Its curved rim sweeps
through the near foreground while one hand holds the connected shaft and the
other steadies it higher up. Reflected mint, lavender, and pale coral lights
form a lively diagonal without floating decorations.

### Foreground Half 08 — Roller Brake

At a smooth indoor rink, one pastel roller skate and its four coherent wheels
push toward the camera as Akari performs a controlled heel brake. Her upper
body counters the leg diagonal, both arms balance naturally, and the second
skate remains grounded. Clothing is adult sporty and fully modest.

### Full Action 09 — Picnic Cloth Snap

In a bright park, Akari and both connected hands snap open one mint, butter,
and cream picnic cloth. The cloth creates a large diagonal foreground wave
while her full adult figure and grounded feet remain readable behind it. Wind
tips the two ears in different directions.

### Full Action 10 — Bowling Release

From a very low lane-level camera, one pastel lavender bowling ball rolls into
the foreground after a coherent release. Akari finishes the motion behind it
with one planted foot and one trailing leg, wearing a cream polo and
mint high-waisted trousers. No readable scoreboard or logos appear.

### Full Action 11 — Kite Reel Turn

On a breezy seaside promenade, Akari turns back while holding one coherent kite
reel with both hands. One continuous string exits upward on a strong diagonal;
the kite may remain outside the crop. Her coral windbreaker and milk-blue midi
skirt move with the wind while the asymmetric ears react differently.

### Full Action 12 — Jump-Rope Cross-Step

Akari performs one controlled cross-step with one continuous lavender jump
rope forming a large arc around her full figure. The handles remain connected
to the rope and separately held. A cream-and-peach athletic outfit uses adult
proportions, knee-covering bottoms, and fully grounded foot placement.

## QA and Iteration

Inspect every final candidate at original detail. A hard failure includes any
breach of canonical identity, adult age, hairpin/ponytail laterality, exact ear
count and natural-ear construction, hidden-human-ear rule, tail prohibition,
modesty, hand or limb anatomy, scene-specific prop count/contact, or physical
continuity. A hard failure may receive one targeted correction that preserves
the rest of the image; unresolved failures must be reported.

The final review must also judge whether each result truly reaches the requested
pop finish, milky-pastel color separation, and dynamic composition rather than
merely adding cat ears to an ordinary portrait. Confirm readable RGB PNGs,
prompt provenance, final file correspondence, and a contact sheet assembled
from the saved finals.
