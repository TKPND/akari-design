# Akari V2.2 Cat-Ear Fantasy Study Design

## Goal

Create six noncanonical Akari V2.2 illustrations that explore a deliberately
"azatoi" adult charm through naturally grown cat ears, close gaze, ear
reactions, and confident everyday gestures. The work must remain recognizably
Akari before it reads as a cat-ear variation.

## Output Boundary

- Produce six portrait PNG candidates at 1024 x 1536.
- Store them under
  `tmp/akari-v2.2-cat-ear-fantasy-study-20260811/r01/`.
- Keep the batch untracked and noncanonical until the user explicitly accepts
  one image.
- Preserve prompts and a six-up contact sheet beside the candidates.
- Do not assign a Daily Scene number or edit the V2.2 accepted package during
  exploration.

## Reference Contract

Every generation must use, not merely mention, the following local reference
roles:

1. `akari-v2.2/accepted/base/akari-v2.2-single-hairpin-portrait.webp` is the
   sole authority for the adult face, amber eyes, chestnut hair, bangs, blue
   capsule hairpin, and apparent age.
2. `akari-v2.2/accepted/base/akari-v2.2-single-hairpin-fullbody.webp` is the
   authority for adult proportions, balance, and laterality.
3. One applicable image from
   `local-reference-packs/akari-v2.2-cute-composition/` supplies only close
   camera grammar, diagonal flow, gentle foreshortening, foreground action,
   and compact color rhythm.
4. `akari-v2.2/accepted/daily/life/akari-v2.2-d07-fan-breeze.png` may be added
   as a natural-motion and physically coherent room anchor.

The accepted authorities win every identity conflict. The local pack must not
donate a face, hairstyle, outfit, ear design, pose, prop arrangement, symbol
field, poster layout, or other recognizable content.

## Shared Character Lock

- Akari is a 25-year-old adult with her canonical warm chestnut hair and amber
  eyes.
- Exactly one filled blue capsule hairpin appears on canvas-right,
  corresponding to Akari's anatomical left.
- Exactly one low side ponytail appears on the same canvas-right side, tied
  with exactly one blue elastic. No braid, twin tails, or second ponytail.
- Exactly two naturally grown cat ears emerge organically from the crown.
  Their outer fur matches the chestnut hair and their inner fur is restrained
  warm pink.
- The ordinary human-ear positions are fully covered by hair; no human ears
  are visible.
- No headband, seams, hardware, costume ear base, tail, collar, bell, paw
  gloves, whiskers, animal nose, claws, or other animal traits.
- Adult proportions and modest adult clothing are mandatory. No mini skirt,
  cleavage, underwear emphasis, school uniform, or childlike age drift.
- "Azatoi" is expressed through direct gaze, a small knowing smile, ear
  angle, camera proximity, and adult confidence rather than exposure.

## Six Scenes

### 01 — Book Peek

In a bright Sunday living room, Akari sits on a low sofa in an oatmeal ribbed
mock-neck top and burgundy wide-leg trousers. One plain burgundy hardcover
book hides the lower part of her face while she looks upward directly at the
viewer with a knowing smile. Each hand holds a different lower corner of the
single book. A low close camera makes the book a strong foreground shape; one
cat ear turns toward the viewer before her face does.

### 02 — Arcade Win

In a coherent night retro arcade, Akari wears a bottle-green bomber jacket, a
white high-neck top, and charcoal tapered trousers. One hand holds exactly one
joystick while the other rests on exactly one large button. The cabinet corner
is a low foreground anchor and she looks back over her shoulder with a coy
"did you see that?" victory expression. Cyan and magenta machine light remain
material light, not a flat graphic field.

### 03 — Rooftop Lights

At blue hour on a rooftop terrace, Akari wears a saffron cardigan over a navy
below-knee shirt dress with brown ankle boots. From a rear three-quarter full
body view, she uses one clip to secure one continuous bulb cord to a readable
railing. She looks back over her canvas-right shoulder, preserving the pin and
ponytail view; her ears react before her soft "help me?" smile. The cord must
remain attached and spatially coherent.

### 04 — Camera Walk

On an evening riverside walk, Akari wears an olive field jacket, cream knit
top, and rust midi skirt. A compact camera and its lens form the low circular
foreground; both hands support the single camera while one continuous strap
connects it to her neck. She lowers the camera just enough to return the
viewer's gaze with a small eyebrow-led smile, and one ear angles forward.

### 05 — Honest Ears

In a clean morning office elevator hall, Akari wears a charcoal adult trouser
suit, muted mauve blouse, and leather loafers. Framed from head to above the
knees, one hand fastens one jacket button while the other holds one thin plain
folio. Her face is professionally composed but one ear tilts with a teasing
"do I look put together?" honesty. No office text or logos are visible.

### 06 — Good Key Sound

At a bright home-work desk, Akari wears a cobalt rolled-sleeve shirt and sand
high-waisted trousers. One graphite keyboard with exactly one coral key fills
the desk-level diagonal foreground. One index finger presses the coral key
while the other hand supports the case edge. Her face stays close and direct,
both ears aiming toward the click with a proud "heard that?" smile. No screen
text or loose duplicate keys appear.

## Rendering and Composition

- Use polished Akari V2.2 anime illustration rendering with refined amber
  eyes, clean linework, warm cheek finish, natural material shading, and
  physically coherent spaces.
- Keep the face a primary focal point even in the wider scenes.
- Use close or gently wide-angle perspective, diagonal energy, and one clear
  foreground action without copying a local-pack layout.
- Backgrounds are real, simple spaces with readable surfaces, contacts, and
  depth. No flat poster background, sticker outline, decorative stars, hearts,
  geometric symbol fields, text, logo, watermark, speech balloon, or comic
  punctuation.

## QA and Iteration

Inspect every final candidate at original detail. A hard failure is any breach
of the identity lock, ear count/type, hidden-human-ear rule, tail prohibition,
adult age/modesty, hand anatomy, specified prop count/contact, or coherent
space. A hard failure receives at most one targeted correction that preserves
the rest of the image. Report unresolved defects instead of silently treating
them as passes.

The final review must confirm all six files are readable 1024 x 1536 RGB PNGs,
the prompt record names every input role, and the contact sheet corresponds to
the saved final files.
