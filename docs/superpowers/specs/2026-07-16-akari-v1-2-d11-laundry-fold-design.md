# Akari v1.2 Daily.3 D11 Laundry Fold Design

## Scope

Create the first Daily Wave 3 life-action scene. Akari folds one plain towel
on the rug in her compact room during soft late-afternoon light. The action
must read as ordinary household work rather than posing, fatigue, or a second
narrative beat.

## Scene contract

- Use a gently elevated front-left three-quarter portrait camera that shows
  the complete seated body, both working hands, the towel, and rug support.
- Akari sits with both knees folded modestly toward character-right. Her hips
  and outer legs are fully supported, both leg chains remain traceable, and
  both striped socked feet remain visible without pointing at the viewer.
- She leans forward only slightly and folds one small pale-blue rectangular
  towel flat on the rug. Each hand holds a different near corner with five
  natural fingers and neutral wrists; the towel must not hide either hand.
- Keep normal short-bob hair, the complete character-left ornament, both eyes
  open and focused down at the towel, and a small relaxed closed mouth.
- Carry exactly two Humanization details: the character-right hoodie cuff is
  pushed up one thumb width and one already-folded warm-white towel sits a
  little askew beside a low plain laundry basket.
- Allow only the rug, one low plain basket, the working towel, and the single
  folded towel. Do not add loose clothing, underwear, text, or another action.

## Reference roles

1. D10 controls recent adult identity, standard hoodie/skirt/socks, body
   volume, and restrained room rendering, but not its sleepy state or lying
   pose.
2. D02 controls only rug material, floor context, and contact shadow, not its
   morning state or extended-leg pose.
3. C03 hairpin-side 45 controls three-quarter identity and the complete
   character-left ornament.
4. C04 controls floor-seated torso/pelvis volume and modest side-folded leg
   readability, not its relaxed hand placement or smile.
5. C07 seated socks controls sock construction, ankle volume, heel, and toe
   shape, not its exact pose.

## Selection and release

Generate independent A/B candidates from only the five accepted references.
Akari selects quality-first under delegated authority. Generate C only if A
and B both have distinct candidate-local Major failures. Promote byte-for-byte,
record ordered reviews, and pass the D11 edit and Natural Form integration
gates with Core unchanged.
