# Akari v1.2 Daily.3 D15 PC Posture Break Design

## Scope

Create the fifth and final Daily Wave 3 life-action scene. Akari relaxes her
posture in front of one desktop computer after a focused task. The pose must
read as an ordinary awake posture break with stable chair and desk support,
never as sleep, illness, collapse, distress, or a second narrative action.

## Scene contract

- Use a gently elevated front three-quarter portrait camera showing Akari's
  complete seated body, the chair support, the near desk edge, one desktop
  monitor, and both socked feet.
- Seat Akari fully on one plain armless desk chair beside one compact light-wood
  desk. Angle the chair slightly toward the camera so neither the desk nor chair
  hides her pelvis, legs, heels, or toes.
- Rest the elbow closest to the desk on the clear near desk corner. That hand
  lightly supports the side of her cheek without covering an eye or mouth. The
  other hand rests open on the corresponding thigh; neither hand operates a
  device or holds a prop.
- Keep both eyes open toward one dark blank unreadable monitor. Use a small
  closed neutral mouth, healthy cheek color, and an awake neck angle.
- Preserve full pelvis and lower-back chair support. Allow one mild sideways
  torso lean and relaxed uneven shoulders, but no sliding, dangling, face-down
  contact, torso-on-desk weight, or unsupported head drop.
- Keep both legs independently traceable and both striped socked feet fully flat
  on the floor in a relaxed stagger.
- Carry exactly two Humanization details: one small diagonal seated fold in the
  hoodie hem and one plain keyboard slightly askew. Add only one plain mouse;
  show no readable UI, tower, cable, phone, drink, food, medicine, clock, paper,
  bag, or extra device.

## Reference roles

1. D14 controls recent adult identity, standard outfit, healthy body volume,
   focused everyday presence, and restrained daylight rendering, but not its
   sofa, bag, notebook, or unpacking action.
2. D13 controls compact light-wood desk family and room scale, but not its open
   drawer, charger, cable, organizer, or standing search pose.
3. D11 r02 controls stable seated pelvis support, separate knees and shins, and
   bilateral flat socked feet, but not its sofa, towel, or laundry action.
4. C03 hairpin-side 45 controls three-quarter identity and the complete
   character-left ornament.
5. C07 seated socks controls seated sock, ankle, heel, toe, and knit
   construction, but not its floor pose or white backdrop.

## Selection and release

Generate independent A/B candidates from only the five accepted references.
Akari selects quality-first under delegated authority. Generate C only if A
and B both have distinct candidate-local Major failures. Promote byte-for-byte,
record ordered reviews, and pass the D15 edit and Natural Form integration
gates with Core unchanged.
