# Akari v1.2 Daily.2 D10 Evening Rug Side Rest Design

## Scope

Create the fifth and final Daily Wave 2 scene. After D09's phone sleepiness,
Akari has put the phone away and lies down safely on a plain rug in the same
compact room. This closes the return-home-to-rest sequence without depicting
sleep, collapse, illness, or distress.

## Scene contract

- Use a gently elevated front-left three-quarter camera that shows the full
  side-lying body and all support contacts.
- Akari lies on her character-right side with the character-left ornament and
  face visible. A single plain low cushion supports her temple; the lower arm
  bends naturally near the cushion and the upper hand rests open on the rug.
- Keep both eyes visibly open under heavy lids, gaze resting slightly down and
  away from the viewer, with a small closed neutral mouth.
- Keep the pelvis and ribcage fully supported by the rug, the upper knee only
  slightly forward, both legs separately traceable, and both striped socked
  feet fully visible with relaxed toes.
- Carry exactly two D09 Humanization details: the character-right cuff remains
  pushed up about one thumb width and the character-right sock remains slightly
  lower with both stripes complete.
- Use only a plain rug and one plain cushion. The phone is now out of frame; do
  not add a second narrative action or explanatory prop.

## Reference roles

1. D09 controls same-evening identity, outfit, room palette, sleepy-awake state,
   cuff, and sock asymmetry, but not its bed sit, phone, or hand pose.
2. D02 controls only plain-rug material, restrained floor context, and readable
   body-to-rug contact, not its morning lighting or seated pose.
3. C03 hairpin-side 45 controls three-quarter identity and the complete
   character-left ornament.
4. C06-1 controls only the visibly-awake sleepy expression intensity.
5. C07 seated socks controls sock construction, ankle volume, heel, and toe
   shape, not its floor-sitting pose.

## Selection and release

Generate independent A/B candidates from only the five accepted references.
Akari selects quality-first under delegated authority. Generate C only if A
and B both have distinct candidate-local Major failures. Promote byte-for-byte,
record ordered reviews, pass the D10 edit and Natural Form integration gates,
then close Wave 2 in the Daily.2 release register with Core unchanged.
