# Akari v1.2 Daily.3 D12 Fridge Open Design

## Scope

Create the second Daily Wave 3 life-action scene. Akari opens one compact
refrigerator in her kitchenette during soft neutral daytime light. The image
must read as one ordinary household action, without retrieving food, holding a
second prop, or turning the appliance into the subject.

## Scene contract

- Use a gently elevated front-left three-quarter portrait camera that shows
  Akari from complete ornament and crown through both socked heels and toes.
- Put the compact plain refrigerator at image-left and Akari immediately to
  its right. She opens the door about 30 to 40 degrees toward image-left with
  her character-right hand, so the door never crosses her body or hides a leg.
- Keep both feet flat and independently traceable in a small natural stagger,
  with stable pelvis, knees, ankles, heels, and toes. Her free hand rests open
  beside the hoodie seam and performs no second action.
- Direct both open eyes toward the middle refrigerator shelf with relaxed
  brows and a small closed, quietly curious mouth. Preserve normal short-bob
  hair and the complete character-left ornament.
- Carry exactly two Humanization details: the character-right hoodie cuff is
  pushed up one thumb width and the character-right sock sits slightly lower
  than the other while retaining exactly two pale-blue stripes.
- Allow only the refrigerator and two or three indistinct blank containers
  inside. Do not add readable labels, loose food, a held object, or another
  action.

## Reference roles

1. D11 controls recent adult identity, standard hoodie/skirt/socks, body
   volume, and restrained daylight rendering, but not sitting or laundry.
2. D04 controls only compact kitchenette scale and ordinary appliance-route
   context, not its alternate outfit, drink, or gait.
3. C03 hairpin-side 45 controls three-quarter identity and the complete
   character-left ornament.
4. C01 controls standing ribcage, pelvis, and leg proportions, not its white
   backdrop or smile.
5. C07 standing socks controls bilateral sock, ankle, heel, toe, and flat-foot
   construction, not its exact stance.

## Selection and release

Generate independent A/B candidates from only the five accepted references.
Akari selects quality-first under delegated authority. Generate C only if A
and B both have distinct candidate-local Major failures. Promote byte-for-byte,
record ordered reviews, and pass the D12 edit and Natural Form integration
gates with Core unchanged.
