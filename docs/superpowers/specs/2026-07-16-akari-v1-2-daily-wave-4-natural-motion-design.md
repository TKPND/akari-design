# Akari v1.2 Daily Wave 4 Natural Motion Design

## Goal

Akari v1.2 Daily Wave 4 adds five standing and in-motion household scenes,
D16 through D20. The wave fills the main coverage gap left after the accepted
morning, evening-rest, and household-action scenes in D01-D15: Akari should
remain recognizably herself while standing, shifting weight, bending, stepping,
and applying force during ordinary life.

The wave preserves the v1.2 Natural Form principle:

> v1.1 が「立ち絵として正しいあかり」なら、v1.2 は「生活している途中でも正しいあかり」。

## Context

Daily Waves 1-3 are complete and D01-D15 are accepted. Wave 4 is a new explicit
design contract and must not be appended silently to the Daily.3 release
register.

Existing accepted coverage is strongest in morning states, floor sitting,
bed and sofa rest, and small household actions. Wave 4 therefore prioritizes
standing balance and whole-body motion instead of adding more seated or resting
variants.

## Scope

- Create and review five portrait illustrations, D16-D20.
- Increase movement difficulty gradually from a grounded turn to a forceful
  fitted-sheet pull.
- Move through several rooms in the home while keeping one common outfit.
- Use one primary motion problem per image.
- Generate each scene independently from Accepted Core references.
- Keep Core identity, body, palette, and accepted image bytes unchanged.

## Non-goals

- New character design, body type, hairstyle, or basic palette.
- New fashion or costume exploration.
- Outdoor, rain, festival, or travel scenes.
- A continuous animation or strict frame-to-frame room layout.
- A collage, contact sheet, split screen, or multi-panel image.
- Promotion of any Wave 4 image into Core.
- Background spectacle, dramatic cinematography, or model-like posing.

## Shared Character and Wardrobe Lock

All five scenes use the same relaxed indoor outfit:

- loose white short-sleeve T-shirt
- gray lounge shorts with clear roomwear construction
- white socks with two pale-blue stripes
- no shoes

Preserve the accepted Akari identity:

- 25-year-old adult impression
- short warm-brown bob
- parallel pin and small pale-blue ribbon on character-left
- warm brown eyes
- compact anime proportions
- sturdy healthy legs with fuller thighs and soft calves
- soft anime rendering
- natural joints and weight balance

Permitted humanization is limited to one or two elements per scene:

- small motion-driven fabric wrinkles
- a lightly displaced T-shirt hem
- a subtle left-right sock-height difference
- slight shoulder, hip, knee, or foot asymmetry caused by the action

## Canvas and Framing

- portrait 2:3 aspect ratio
- single standalone image
- one scene and one composition
- Akari occupies approximately 60-70% of the frame
- retain the full head, both hands, and both feet
- avoid extreme wide-angle perspective, dramatic low angles, and strong
  foreshortening
- keep the face, pelvis, knees, ankles, and support surfaces readable
- backgrounds explain the action but do not visually dominate Akari

## Scene Set

| Asset | Place and action | Primary body problem | Load |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D16 | Turn toward a call at the bedroom doorway | linked head, chest, pelvis, and staggered feet | light |
| D17 | Reach toward the upper shelf of a hall closet | one-sided extension, slight heel lift, asymmetric shoulders | light-medium |

## D16: Doorway Turn

### D16 story

Akari has just been called from another room. She has stopped at the bedroom
doorway and turns back with a quiet, soft “ん？” reaction. The image captures
the turn in progress rather than a completed pose.

### D16 camera and composition

- eye-level three-quarter view from inside the bedroom
- use the doorway as a vertical frame
- place Akari slightly off center
- keep one foot slightly ahead of the other
- keep both feet grounded and fully visible

### D16 acceptance focus

- the neck, chest, and pelvis turn as one plausible chain
- the staggered feet explain the turn
- weight remains grounded rather than mannequin-stiff
- the expression is gentle and responsive, not surprised or theatrical

### D16 reject when

- only the head twists
- the pelvis and feet face incompatible directions
- the stance becomes a fashion pose
- the doorway hides a hand, knee, ankle, or foot

## D17: Upper-Shelf Reach

### D17 story

In the hallway, Akari reaches toward the upper shelf of a storage closet while
looking for an ordinary household item. The action is a small stretch, not a
dramatic tiptoe pose.

### D17 camera and composition

- waist-height three-quarter side view from farther down the hallway
- use the closet verticals to show the extension
- include the reaching fingertips and both feet
- allow only a slight heel lift on the reaching side

### D17 acceptance focus

- one side of the torso extends without lengthening the entire body
- the shoulder line is naturally asymmetric
- the planted foot and lightly raised heel explain the load
- the gaze remains directed into the shelf

### D17 reject when

- both feet rise into a ballet-like tiptoe
- the waist becomes unnaturally narrow or elongated
- the reaching arm disconnects from the shoulder
- the pose reads as performance rather than household life

## D18: Lower-Cabinet Retrieve

### D18 story

In the kitchen, Akari bends to take a container from a lower cabinet. Her gaze
has found the object, and the motion remains calm and purposeful.

### D18 camera and composition

- light high-angle three-quarter view from the opposite side of the kitchen
- keep the pelvis, knees, and ankles from overlapping
- show the cabinet opening and target container without clutter
- do not use furniture to hide the joint chain

### D18 acceptance focus

- the movement begins at the hips rather than collapsing only at the waist
- knees and toes point in compatible directions
- both feet have plausible contact and pressure
- the face remains readable without forcing it toward the camera

### D18 reject when

- the back folds sharply while the legs remain rigid
- one knee or ankle rotates inward beyond a natural range
- the cabinet masks the lower body
- the body becomes a deep squat unrelated to the intended reach

## D19: Cable Step-over

### D19 story

While crossing the living room, Akari notices a charging cable and carefully
steps over it. The image captures the instant after one foot is planted and the
other clears the cable.

### D19 camera and composition

- side-biased view from slightly above floor level
- show the cable, support leg, and clearing foot in a readable sequence
- keep the arms free for quiet natural balance
- use no carried prop that competes with the gait problem

### D19 acceptance focus

- the planted leg visibly carries the body
- the lifted leg belongs to a walking step, not a running or dance pose
- the pelvis follows the direction of travel
- the cautious expression stays small and everyday

### D19 reject when

- both feet appear airborne
- the support knee hyperextends
- the lifted foot points like ballet
- the cable wraps around, touches, or visually merges with the ankle
- the pose reads as jumping or running

## D20: Fitted-Sheet Pull

### D20 story

Back in the bedroom, Akari pulls a fitted-sheet corner into place. She applies
visible but ordinary effort, with a staggered stance and the whole body helping
the arms.

### D20 camera and composition

- light high-angle three-quarter side view from the bed corner
- arrange hands, inclined torso, and staggered feet along a diagonal
- keep both hands and both feet visible
- show only enough of the bed to establish the fitted-sheet resistance

### D20 acceptance focus

- force travels from the planted feet through pelvis and back to both arms
- the front and rear legs have different, understandable roles
- shoulders show effort without rising into a shrug
- expression shows mild concentration rather than exaggerated strain

### D20 reject when

- only the arms appear to pull
- the feet slide or point in unrelated directions
- the stance becomes an extreme lunge or wide split
- bed geometry obscures the pelvis, knees, or feet
- the pose becomes glamorous, athletic, or theatrical

## Generation Contract

Every request must state:

> single standalone image, one scene only, one composition only, no collage,
> no grid, no split screen, no multi-panel, no contact sheet.

Generate D16 through D20 sequentially. Complete selection and acceptance for
one asset before beginning the next asset. Do not use the preceding Daily image
as the next image-generation reference. Re-anchor each asset independently to
the accepted v1.2 Core identity, body, face-hair, and indoor-feet references.

If a retry is needed, change one primary prompt dimension only. Preserve earlier
candidates and record why the selected candidate is stronger.

## Review Order

Review every candidate in this order:

1. Identity: same Akari, adult age impression, face, bob, and ornament side.
2. Body: healthy leg volume, coherent joints, support point, and weight path.
3. Motion: the action reads before props or background details are examined.
4. State: expression and clothing response match the physical action.
5. Rendering: soft anime treatment, restrained light, and readable anatomy.
6. Production: 2:3 portrait, one scene, full extremities, no text or logos.

## Common Blockers

Reject a candidate with any of the following:

- different character or childlike age impression
- mirrored, incomplete, or duplicated hair ornament
- thin legs, elongated proportions, or model stance
- disconnected limbs, extra digits, extra limbs, or hidden critical joints
- exaggerated splits, ballet feet, hyperextended support knee, or implausible
  single-leg balance
- sexualized camera, clothing, anatomy, or expression
- readable text, logo, watermark, collage, or multi-panel layout
- dramatic cinematic lighting that hides the body mechanics

## Wave Acceptance

Wave 4 is complete only when:

- D16-D20 each have one accepted revision
- each accepted image has candidate review evidence and selected-source linkage
- every scene passes its declared image-production and Daily lifecycle checks
- Core assets and Core image bytes remain unchanged
- the Daily handoff points to a separate Wave 4 release register
- no unresolved Blocker or Major body-motion issue remains

## Expected Release Boundary

Wave 4 should register its accepted scenes in a new
`akari-v1.2-daily-wave-4.md` document. It must not modify the completed Wave 3
register except for any deliberately separate navigation link approved during
implementation.

This document defines the Wave 4 image and review contract only. It does not
authorize image generation, asset registration, release automation, or Core
changes by itself.
