# Akari v1.2 Daily.3 D13 Charger Search Design

## Scope

Create the third Daily Wave 3 life-action scene. Akari searches one shallow
desk drawer for her charger in soft neutral daytime light. The action stays at
the moment before retrieval: the adapter and cable remain in the drawer and
neither hand holds a found item.

## Scene contract

- Use a gently elevated front-right three-quarter portrait camera showing the
  complete standing body, open drawer, both hands, and both socked feet.
- Put one compact plain desk at image-left and Akari immediately to its right.
  Her character-right hand pulls the shallow waist-height drawer toward her;
  her free character-left hand hovers open above its contents.
- Keep both feet fully flat in a small natural stagger and every leg joint
  independently traceable. The drawer must not cross her torso or hide a leg.
- Direct both open eyes down into the drawer with a small closed, mildly
  puzzled but calm mouth. Preserve normal short-bob hair and the complete
  character-left ornament.
- Carry exactly two Humanization details: the character-right hoodie cuff is
  pushed up one thumb width and one small plain drawer organizer is slightly
  askew.
- Show only one blank adapter, one short loosely coiled pale-blue cable, and
  one closed blank notebook inside the drawer. Add no phone, held item, labels,
  extra cable, or second action.

## Reference roles

1. D12 controls recent adult identity, standard outfit, upright household
   action, and restrained daylight rendering, but not its refrigerator.
2. D04 controls only compact-room route scale and ordinary standing movement,
   not its alternate outfit, drink, or kitchen fixtures.
3. C03 hairpin-side 45 controls three-quarter identity and the complete
   character-left ornament.
4. C01 controls standing ribcage, pelvis, and bilateral leg proportions.
5. C07 standing socks controls bilateral sock, ankle, heel, toe, and flat-foot
   construction.

## Selection and release

Generate independent A/B candidates from only the five accepted references.
Akari selects quality-first under delegated authority. Generate C only if A
and B both have distinct candidate-local Major failures. Promote byte-for-byte,
record ordered reviews, and pass the D13 edit and Natural Form integration
gates with Core unchanged.
