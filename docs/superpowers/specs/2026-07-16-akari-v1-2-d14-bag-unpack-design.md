# Akari v1.2 Daily.3 D14 Bag Unpack Design

## Scope

Create the fourth Daily Wave 3 life-action scene. Akari unpacks one pale soft
shoulder tote while seated at the edge of a compact sofa in soft neutral
daytime light. The image freezes one clear unpacking moment: one notebook is
halfway out, while two other contents already rest beside the bag.

## Scene contract

- Use a gently elevated front three-quarter portrait camera showing the
  complete seated body, open bag, both hands, and both socked feet.
- Seat Akari shallowly on the screen-right half of one compact sofa. Place one
  upright open bag on the adjacent screen-left cushion, fully supported and
  never resting on her lap.
- The screen-left hand holds the near bag rim open. The screen-right hand lifts
  one closed blank gray notebook vertically halfway out; neither hand performs
  a second action.
- Keep knees separate, shins independently traceable, and both socked feet fully
  flat on one small rug with complete heels and toes. The bag and contents must
  not cross her torso, skirt, legs, or feet.
- Direct both open eyes toward the notebook and bag with a small closed,
  quietly focused mouth. Preserve normal short-bob hair and the complete
  character-left ornament.
- Carry exactly two Humanization details: the bag strap rests in one loose curve
  on its cushion, and one small pale-blue zip pouch lies slightly askew.
- Show exactly three bag contents: the notebook being lifted, the askew pouch,
  and one neatly folded warm-white handkerchief already on the cushion. Add no
  phone, wallet, keys, charger, cable, food, drink, labels, or second bag.

## Reference roles

1. D13 controls recent adult identity, standard outfit, focused household
   action, and restrained daylight rendering, but not its desk or drawer.
2. D11 r02 controls shallow sofa-edge pelvis support, separate knees and shins,
   and bilateral flat socked feet, but not laundry or towel handling.
3. D06 controls only the accepted pale soft bag palette, material, scale, and

4. C03 hairpin-side 45 controls three-quarter identity and the complete

5. C07 seated socks controls seated sock, ankle, heel, toe, and knit

## Selection and release

Generate independent A/B candidates from only the five accepted references.
Akari selects quality-first under delegated authority. Generate C only if A
and B both have distinct candidate-local Major failures. Promote byte-for-byte,
record ordered reviews, and pass the D14 edit and Natural Form integration
