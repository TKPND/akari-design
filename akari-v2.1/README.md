# Akari v2.1 Stable-Eye Front Baseline

Status: front face master and front full-body baseline promoted.

Date: 2026-08-05.

Akari v2.1 is a bounded refinement of the accepted v2.0 character. It keeps
the same-person lineage and signature casual silhouette while using a quieter,
more stable eye construction and a cleaner no-drawcord shorts design.

## Canonical Authorities

- `accepted/base/akari-v2.1-front-face-master.png` is the sole high-detail
  authority for face identity, eye construction, cheeks, chin, expression,
  hair, ornament, ponytail, skin treatment, and close-view rendering.
- `accepted/base/akari-v2.1-front-fullbody.png` is the canonical strict-front
  authority for proportions, neutral stance, outfit, utility pocket, socks,
  shoes, laterality, and complete-figure presentation.

When drawing scale creates a visible difference, the face master wins for
facial and close-view identity decisions. The full-body image wins for body
balance, outfit construction, and front-view laterality.

## Locked Design

- Approachable 18-year-old young adult with a fresh, familiar presence.
- Warm chestnut hair, off-center V bangs, honey-amber almond eyes, and a
  pale-blue crossed hairpin.
- Low side ponytail and hairpin on character-left. In the canonical front view,
  both appear on canvas-right.
- Medium-width eyes with restrained vertical opening, subtle pupils, and a
  simple highlight treatment.
- Soft cheeks, compact chin, and a small open friendly smile.
- Compact healthy body with moderate shoulders and torso, restrained bust,
  slight waist definition, and healthy thighs and calves.
- Plain white compact-fit crew-neck T-shirt.
- Navy soft A-line mid-thigh shorts with no drawcord.
- One slim vertical utility pocket on character-left/canvas-right with a
  minimal warm-metal zipper detail.
- White short socks and rounded everyday blue-and-white sneakers.
- Warm off-white background with a restrained grounding shadow.

## Generation Use

Open both canonical images before identity-sensitive generation. Use the face
master as the primary identity reference and the full-body image as the body,
outfit, laterality, and full-figure reference. Do not let a supporting body or
pose reference replace the face master's identity.

## Scope Boundary

This is a minimal two-image character-design checkpoint. The local 30-degree
stability probe is noncanonical evidence only. This package is not a turnaround,
angle set, expression sheet, wardrobe set, uniform batch, manifest-backed
release, or PDF. Candidates, comparisons, prompts, and auxiliary views remain
untracked.
