# Akari v2.0 Cute Front Baseline

Status: front face master and front full-body baseline promoted.

Date: 2026-08-03.

Akari v2.0 is the approved cute-direction redesign. It uses one high-detail
front face master to protect identity and one strict-front full-body image to
define the practical body, outfit, laterality, and presentation baseline.

## Canonical Authorities

- `accepted/base/akari-v2.0-front-face-master.png` — sole high-detail authority
  for the face, eye construction, cheeks, chin, open smile, hair, ornament,
  ponytail, skin treatment, and close-view rendering.
- `accepted/base/akari-v2.0-front-fullbody.png` — canonical strict-front
  full-body authority for proportions, neutral stance, outfit, organizer,
  socks, shoes, and full-figure presentation.

If the two images appear to differ because of drawing scale, the face master
wins for all facial and close-view identity decisions. The full-body image wins
for body balance, outfit construction, and front-view laterality.

## Locked Design

- 18-year-old young adult with a cute, approachable childhood-friend presence.
- Warm chestnut hair, off-center V bangs, amber eyes, and a pale-blue crossed
  ornament.
- Low side ponytail and ornament on character-left. In the canonical front
  view, both appear on canvas-right.
- Soft cheeks, compact rounded chin, and the face master's open friendly smile.
- Compact healthy body with moderate shoulders and torso, restrained bust,
  subtle waist, natural arms, and full healthy thighs and calves.
- Plain white T-shirt and navy A-line mid-thigh shorts.
- Exactly two short drawcord ends with no bow.
- One slim vertical navy organizer on character-left/canvas-right with a tiny
  warm-metal zipper pull.
- White socks and generic blue-and-white sneakers.
- Warm off-white background and restrained grounding shadow.

## Generation Use

Open both canonical images before identity-sensitive generation. Use the face
master as the primary identity reference and the full-body image as the body,
outfit, and laterality reference. Oblique or rear views may be inferred by the
image-generation workflow, but they do not become canonical without a separate
explicit promotion decision.

## Scope Boundary

This is a minimal two-image character-design checkpoint. It is not a complete
turnaround, paired angle set, rear-view package, jacket variation, daily-pose
gallery, manifest-backed release, or PDF. Generated candidates, comparisons,
GPT Pro transfer packages, and auxiliary views remain untracked.
