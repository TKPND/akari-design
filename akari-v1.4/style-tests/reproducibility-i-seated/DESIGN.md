# Akari v1.4 — I seated reproducibility design

Date: 2026-07-23

## Carried decision

The user adopted `H-r03-1` without further mechanical scaling. Its near-full
portrait framing is accepted as a valid standing composition rather than a
defect to be corrected. The proposed H-r04 normalized-reference retry is
cancelled.

The broader reproducibility sequence remains:

1. standing;
2. seated;
3. action in progress.

I is the seated stage.

## Approaches considered

### A — armless-chair seated view

Akari sits naturally on a simple armless pale-oak chair. Both feet are flat,
one hand rests lightly on each thigh, and every joint remains visible.

This is selected because it changes the hip, knee, ankle, hand, and
chair-contact anatomy while adding only one simple scene object.

### B — low stool with forward lean

This gives more personality but overlaps the forearms, hands, torso, and
thighs. A failure would be difficult to attribute to style or occlusion.

### C — floor sitting

This gives a strong silhouette but overlaps the existing bed/sofa/rug-rest
family and hides lower-limb structure. It is not used.

## Selected design

- One adult Akari, age 25.
- Portrait 2:3, exact 1024 × 1536.
- Eye-level three-quarter front view from the ornament side.
- A simple pale-oak armless chair is the only scene object.
- Akari sits upright with a small natural forward softness, not a fashion
  pose.
- Her pelvis is centered on the seat.
- Knees and ankles remain separated and uncrossed.
- Both soles and heels rest fully on the floor.
- One relaxed open hand rests on each thigh with visible, unfused fingers.
- Head, both hands, both feet, and the complete chair silhouette remain in
  frame.
- Framing should feel comfortably close and use most of the portrait canvas.
  No numerical 72% scale target or mechanical shrinking is imposed.
- Outfit remains the accepted loose white T-shirt and pale-blue lounge shorts,
  with bare legs and feet.
- Warm frame-left daylight, a warm-gray wall, and a pale matte floor continue
  from H-r03-1.

## Reference authority

1. G2 controls v1.4 paint planes, line hierarchy, hair finish, skin, and the
   grain/bloom-free rendering.
2. H-r03-1 controls the adopted adult face and eye balance, body volume,
   roomwear, and near-full portrait presence.
3. The v1.1 ornament-side three-quarter reference controls only canonical
   character-left ornament topology and placement.

No generated I sample may be used as a reference for another I sample.

## Acceptance gates

At least two of three independent samples must satisfy all of the following:

- adult 25-year-old identity and H-r03-1-like eye balance;
- G2-like large paint planes and quiet line hierarchy;
- canonical crossed pale-blue pins plus one thin cord bow;
- full head, both hands, both feet, and complete chair visible;
- pelvis supported by the seat with coherent hip and thigh anatomy;
- both soles and heels flat on the floor;
- uncrossed knees and ankles;
- human legs visually separable from chair legs;
- healthy thigh and calf volume without stylized slimming;
- accepted white T-shirt and pale-blue lounge shorts;
- no forbidden extra props, text, grain, bloom, or model-pose styling.

## Rejected scene overlap

No bed, sofa, rug-rest pose, rain, POV, wake-up, return-home, refrigerator,
laundry, charger, bag, shelf reach, food-table action, mail, plant care, or
book comparison.
