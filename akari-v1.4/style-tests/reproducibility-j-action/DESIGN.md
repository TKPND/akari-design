## Approaches considered

### A — table-side mandarin peel

Akari sits across a small dining table and peels one mandarin with both hands.
The action is legible but low-load: one small fruit, one continuous peel, and
one plain dish. It changes the gaze, arms, wrists, fingers, and object contact
while retaining the accepted seated adult continuity.

This is selected.

### B — remove an envelope from a communal mailbox

This tests standing reach and a small object, but the mailbox grid, door,
envelope, and exterior or common-area setting create several independent
failure sources.

### C — wipe a houseplant leaf with a cloth

This tests asymmetric two-hand contact, but leaf anatomy, plant volume, cloth,
and occlusion make it difficult to distinguish a hand-action failure from a
scene-object failure.

The book-comparison scene is deferred because two books, two readable
silhouettes, and a bookstore background are the highest-load option.

## Selected design

- One adult Akari, age 25.
- Portrait 2:3, exact 1024 × 1536.
- Eye-level three-quarter front view from the ornament side, seen from the
  opposite side of a small dining table.
- Akari occupies about 65% of the frame and is shown from crown to below the
  knees.
- She remains seated naturally, with both forearms and both hands fully above
  the tabletop.
- One hand supports a single partially peeled mandarin.
- The other hand gently lifts one broad continuous section of peel that
  remains attached to or clearly in contact with the fruit.
- Both wrists are visible. Fingers remain separate from one another and from
  the fruit.
- One small plain cream dish sits near the mandarin. It is the only additional
  tabletop object.
- The background contains only a quiet warm-gray wall and a small visible edge
  of the dining chair.
- Akari wears the accepted loose opaque white short-sleeved T-shirt and
  pale-blue lounge shorts with a restrained drawstring; bare legs, no socks or
  shoes.
- Her amber eyes look down at the fruit, not toward the camera.
- Expression is a relaxed closed mouth with faint warmth and attentive eyes.
- Soft warm morning light enters from frame-left with mild cream tabletop
  bounce. No light source is visible.

## Acceptance gates

At least two of three independent samples must satisfy every controlling gate:

- adult 25-year-old identity and I-2-like calm eye balance;
- G2-like large paint planes and quiet line hierarchy;
- canonical two crossed pale-blue pins above one thin cord bow;
- crown-to-below-knees seated framing with coherent shoulders, elbows, wrists,
  hips, thighs, and knees;
- both wrists and both hands fully visible above the tabletop;
- one hand plausibly supports the single mandarin;
- the other hand plausibly lifts one continuous peel section;
- peel remains attached to or visibly contacting the fruit, not floating;
- fingers remain anatomically plausible, separate, and not merged into fruit;
- gaze is directed at the fruit, not the camera;
- accepted white T-shirt and pale-blue lounge shorts;
- exactly one mandarin and one small plain dish;
- no forbidden extra props, text, grain, bloom, or food-ad staging.

## Rejection conditions

Reject a sample for any of the following:

- knife, fork, drink, mug, breakfast spread, refrigerator, readable packaging,
  second fruit, or any other added object;
- fruit presented toward the viewer, commercial food-ad pose, or direct camera
  gaze;
- detached floating spiral peel, implausible peel direction, hidden wrists,
  fingers fused together, or fingers merged into the fruit;
- visible window, curtain, sunbeam, lens flare, dramatic backlight, or heavy
  depth-of-field blur;
- long hair, compact v1.2 bob, childlike or glamorous drift, missing or
  incorrect ornament, uniformly thin body, or altered roomwear;
- text, logo, watermark, border, collage, grid, split screen, or additional
  character;
- grain, paper texture, bloom, haze, global smoothing, repeated contours,
  scratch-like line fragments, or equally dark interior fold lines.
