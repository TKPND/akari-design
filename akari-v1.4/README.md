# Akari v1.4 Style Baseline

Status: G–J reproducibility sequence complete.

Date: 2026-07-23.

This checkpoint records the v1.4 rendering baseline and its first independent
composition tests. It preserves every compared candidate, including failed
tests that explain why later controls changed.

## Accepted lineage

1. **D2** — medium-strength hair and clothing paint planes.
2. **E2** — balanced crown and upper-hair plane accent.
3. **F2** — canonical crossed-pin and thin-cord ornament with a softened hair
   boundary.
4. **G2** — accepted line hierarchy, large paint planes, palette, and finish.
5. **H-r03-1** — accepted standing composition and adult-face direction.
6. **I-2** — accepted seated composition and face continuity.
7. **J-1** — accepted everyday action-in-progress direction.

The framing gate used during H-r03 was intentionally not carried forward.
H-r03-1's larger figure presence was accepted as the standing composition, and
the proposed mechanical H-r04 reduction was cancelled.

## Fixed rendering constraints

- Adult 25-year-old Akari identity.
- Short airy chestnut bob and one complete character-left ornament: two
  pale-blue crossed pins above a delicate thin cord bow with narrow loops and
  two slim tails.
- Large readable paint planes with thin deliberate outer strokes and quieter
  interior lines.
- Warm skin, clear amber eyes, and healthy body volume.
- No grain, paper texture, bloom, chromatic aberration, or global smoothing.
- Independent samples within each reproducibility test; no output is used as
  the generation reference for a sibling sample.

## Contents

- `style-tests/` contains the C–J candidates, comparisons, exact prompts, test
  designs, evaluations, and selection notes.
- `references/v1.1/` contains the two inherited anatomy and ornament references
  used by the H–J tests.

Akari v1.3 accepted base assets remain unchanged. This package is a durable
v1.4 design checkpoint, not a release PDF.
