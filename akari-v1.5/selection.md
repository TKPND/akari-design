# Akari v1.5 — B3 Body-Balance Selection

Date: 2026-07-29.

## Promoted result

**B3 is the accepted initial body-balance reference for Akari v1.5.**

The comparison tested light, medium, and strong upper-body volume adjustments
while keeping the established healthy thigh volume. B3 was selected from the
medium range because it improves the upper-to-lower-body balance without the
stronger emphasis of the C range.
