# Akari v1.5 Body-Balance Baseline

Status: initial body-balance checkpoint selected.

Date: 2026-07-29.

This checkpoint starts Akari v1.5 by adjusting the upper-body volume to better
balance the established healthy thigh volume. Face, age impression, head-to-body
ratio, leg volume, neutral standing pose, roomwear, and the v1.4 hand-painted
direction remain the intended continuity constraints.

## Selection

Twelve independent candidates were compared in three ranges:

- A1–A4: light adjustment;
- B1–B4: medium adjustment;
- C1–C4: strong adjustment.

**B3 is the user-selected v1.5 body-balance baseline.**

B3 makes the intended upper-body change clearly visible while remaining more
restrained than the C range. Its balance with the thighs was preferred without
promoting the stronger upper-bound variants.
