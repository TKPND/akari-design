AKARI v3.1 — REQUIRED SOCK MATERIAL RENDERING (MUST)

Whenever any sock fabric is visible, preserve the selected soft opaque rib-knit rendering. F09 is the preferred combined reference for the rounded opaque toe envelope and restrained natural cloth wrinkles. By default, supply its unmodified sock-detail crop, references/materials/F09-rounded-wrinkled-sock-detail.png, as the material-only input; the full selected F09 remains available for context. F01/F07 remain rounded-toe comparisons, and F01/F02/F04/F05/F06/F07 remain supporting soft-material examples. Assign each input its role explicitly. Native L1 remains the leg-construction, medium-neutral-grey color, below-knee length and fit reference; G2 remains the sock-color origin.

The visible sock-covered toes MUST retain the F09/F01/F07 shape: one continuous, softly rounded, fully opaque fabric envelope enclosing all toes together. The knit has enough body and ease to bridge over the individual toes. Keep the front contour smooth and broadly rounded, with connected cloth shading and ordinary foot proportions. Individual toes MUST NOT be readable through transparency, linework, shading, or silhouette: no toe-separation grooves, nail shapes, individual toe knuckles or bumps, scalloped toe tips, or a separate big-toe bulge. Do not divide the sock into five toe pockets or a split-toe shape. Absence of skin color alone does not satisfy this rule; the fabric must also conceal individual toe anatomy.

Small folds and compression at the ankles, heels and contact areas MUST respond naturally to the current bend, weight and surface. Keep supple knit and visible rib texture, with soft fabric volume rather than rigid shoe-like bulk. Toe seams, ribs and folds may describe the cloth but must not trace or separate individual toes.

For the visible forefoot and toe-cap surface, MUST include the restrained cloth life selected in F09: a few sparse, shallow, softly shaded compression creases near the forefoot/toe seam, with broad smooth fabric areas between them. Use natural asymmetry and let the crease direction and amount respond to the current pressure and bend. Rounded refers to the continuous outer shape, not a perfectly featureless or rigid surface. Match F09's modest wrinkle strength without copying its exact stance, crease count or placement. Avoid exaggerated deep grooves, repeated padded rolls and folds that outline hidden toes. If this region is covered by shoes or outside the frame, do not force it into view.

After generation, inspect both visible toe areas enlarged against F09, with F01/F07 as shape comparisons. Check the continuous rounded outline, fully opaque fabric, absence of individual toe anatomy, and sparse natural cloth wrinkles separately. Individually sculpted toes fail even when grey and opaque; a rigid featureless cap also misses the selected F09 cloth feel. Check ankle softness and below-knee length as well.

Apply this requirement to the visible parts only. With shoes on, preserve the knit quality on the exposed calf or ankle while allowing the footwear to cover the toe and heel. Do not remove shoes, expose hidden toes, make footwear transparent, or change the framing solely to demonstrate the sock detail. If socks are fully outside the frame or covered, the requirement does not force them into view.

Pose, action, camera and composition are free to vary for new scenes. The selected material examples do not impose their seated pose, room, outfit or facial design. Current scene and wardrobe instructions still determine the composition and clothing. For corrections, the edit target remains the scene, pose and camera source unless a change is explicitly requested.

P1 remains the original face, eyes, black-bob and drawing authority; P2 remains the everyday closed-mouth smile authority; native U1 and L1 remain separate detail references. New full-body scenes with visible socks normally use P1, P2, U1, L1 and one selected material example. An edit target that already has the selected soft material can also supply that material reference; a socks-only correction can use the target, native L1 and a selected material example with other identity/detail inputs as needed.
